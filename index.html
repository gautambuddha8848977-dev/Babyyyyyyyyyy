If you were here today,

this is how it would feel.

I imagine waking up slowly, not rushing anywhere, just knowing you’re beside me. The day would start softer, simply because you exist in it.

We wouldn’t need plans. Sitting together, talking about nothing, sharing silence that feels full — those are the moments I miss the most.

As the day slows down, that’s when I feel your absence the loudest. Not in a sad way — just in the way love notices what it’s missing.

And when night finally comes, I carry you with me into sleep. Distance never changes what’s real — it only reminds me how deeply I love you.

I miss you. I love you. And I love being yours.
